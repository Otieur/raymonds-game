| When | Decision | Why |

| 00:30 | The Opportunist's "heads up" rule takes its bid part from Shark and its buy part from Land-grabber, not from Duelist. | Duelist's behaviour sits in its two-player override, which is not part of any of its seven parts, so taking parts from it would take the baseline's. |

| 00:58 | Each preset plays 2,000 games at each of its sizes in the M2 study, not 20,000.
